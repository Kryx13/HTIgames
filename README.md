# 🏛️ Scape

**Run. Solve. Escape.**

*Scape* is a high-paced, third-person 3D adventure game set deep within a dark and mysterious Aztec pyramid. You have fallen into the depths of an ancient dungeon, and the only way out is up.

Combine precise parkour mechanics with logical puzzle-solving to navigate through 6 distinct stages. There are no weapons to save you—only your agility and your wits.

**Your Goal:** Find the **Amulet** and escape the dungeon as quickly as possible. Every second counts.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

The game supports both QWERTY and AZERTY keyboard layouts.

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| **Move** | `WASD` / `ZQSD` |
| **Look** | `Mouse` |
| **Jump** | `Space` |
| **Sprint** | `Left Shift` |
| **Interact / Pickup** | Automatic (Walk over object) |
| **Pause / Menu** | `Esc` |

## 🗺️ The Challenge: 6 Stages

You must survive six unique trials to reach the surface. Failure means falling back to the beginning.

1. **The Descent (Tutorial):** Learn the basics of movement and interaction.
2. **The Collapse:** A test of agility. Jump across crumbling platforms before they fall into the void.
3. **The Door Maze:** A test of memory. Navigate a confusing labyrinth of identical rooms and teleporting doors.
4. **The Riddle:** A test of logic. decipher the clues to proceed.
5. **The Great Maze:** A test of navigation. Find your path through a massive stone labyrinth.
6. **The Void:** A test of courage. Navigate through total darkness where one wrong step is fatal.

---

## 🎒 The Objective

Unlike traditional adventure games, you have no inventory management and no tools.

* **The Amulet:** This is the only item that matters. Hidden within the dungeon, you must find it to unlock the final exit gate.

---

## 🏆 Key Features

* **Speedrun Focus:** An integrated timer and global leaderboard track your fastest runs. Can you reach the Top 10?
* **Parkour Gameplay:** Fluid movement is essential to survive falling platforms and navigate terrain.
* **Atmospheric Audio:** Immersive sound design with echoing ambience and stone footsteps.
* **Save Your Best Time:** Compete against yourself and others to shave seconds off your record.
